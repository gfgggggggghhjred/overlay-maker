White Xbox controller asset pack

The PNGs use a shared 2048 × 1664 transparent canvas. Draw the layers at
(0, 0) in the back-to-front order listed in layers.json. The reference is
the complete source model rendered in the same orthographic view. The
layered preview shows the exported assets composited in neutral position.

The sticks, sockets and face controls were rendered from the source mesh.
The bumpers and triggers are visible-surface cutouts from the front render.
Their hidden geometry and the surfaces behind them are unavailable in this
view. Use the shoulder cutouts for masked illumination or analog opacity.
Input indices follow the standard Gamepad mapping. A physical-controller
recording was not supplied. Verify button mapping, stick travel and analog
trigger response with the target controller before customer delivery.
